I’m a technology executive and engineer. I currently serve as Chief Technology Officer at [EasyPost](https://www.easypost.com/), where I lead the company’s technology organization and strategy across platform, product, and experience.

My background is in electrical and computer engineering, distributed systems, internet infrastructure, and engineering leadership. Before EasyPost, I held engineering roles at Fastly and Engine Yard.

I’m most interested in problems where technical architecture, organizational design, and economics interact. That includes reliability, developer leverage, infrastructure, logistics networks, and the systems used to make decisions inside complex organizations.

This site is a selective record of that work and the ideas that come out of it.

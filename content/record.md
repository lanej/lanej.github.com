## EasyPost {#easypost-technology-strategy}

**Chief Technology Officer · 2026–present**

I lead company-wide technology strategy across product, platform, data, and infrastructure. My work includes consolidating overlapping products and systems, strengthening data and decision capabilities, and evaluating technology investments against cost, margin, reliability, and risk.

### Earlier scope

- **2025–2026 — Vice President of Engineering.** Led a 130+ engineer organization across Core API, Enterprise, Platform, and international subsidiaries. Focused on portfolio simplification, capital allocation, and operating mechanisms.
- **2022–2025 — platform leadership.** Led infrastructure, finance, shared services, and developer tooling. Redesigned financial data pipelines to shorten revenue-recognition time, alongside architectural reviews, R&D, reliability, and developer-throughput work.
- **2019–2022 — engineering and management.** Built warehouse logistics, inventory, order integrations, and fulfillment software. Later led USPS CASS-certified address verification and the decomposition of a monolithic application into services.

## Engine Yard {#engine-yard}

**Cloud API architecture and development**

I architected and led development of the Engine Yard API, from backend services to the interfaces developers used to operate their applications and infrastructure.

The official Ruby client and command-line utility, `ey-core`, support application deployment and environment management, including a mocked mode for testing integrations without live API calls.
